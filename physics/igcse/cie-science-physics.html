Welcome, students! This specialized section contains study lessons, revision notes, targeted multiple choice & structured practice questions, and official syllabus resources for the Physics components of the Cambridge IGCSE Combined Science (0653) and Cambridge IGCSE Co-ordinated Sciences (Double Award) (0654) qualifications.

[!TIP] Interdisciplinary Study: If you are taking Co-ordinated Sciences (0654) or Combined Science (0653), you can access the Chemistry component resources directly on our sister portal: CIE IGCSE Science_Chemistry (0653 / 0654).

1. Course Introduction

The Physics component of Cambridge IGCSE Combined Science (0653) and Co-ordinated Sciences (0654) covers fundamental principles of physics, mechanics, thermal physics, wave properties, electricity, electromagnetism, and space physics, preparing students to appreciate the physical world and technological applications.

  • Combined Science (0653): A single-award syllabus containing an equal mix of Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. It counts as a single IGCSE grade.
  • Co-ordinated Sciences (0654): A double-award syllabus featuring a more detailed, double-weighted coverage of Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. It counts as two IGCSE grades.

Assessment Outline (Physics Components)

Candidates who have studied the Core syllabus content are entered for Paper 1 (Multiple Choice), Paper 3 (Theory) and either Paper 5 (Practical) or Paper 6 (Alternative to Practical). Candidates who have studied the Extended syllabus content (Core + Supplement) are entered for Paper 2 (Multiple Choice), Paper 4 (Theory) and either Paper 5 or Paper 6.

Syllabus Structure

The Physics component comprises 6 major topic areas:

NoteTopic 1: Motion, Forces & Energy

Physical quantities and measurement techniques; motion; mass, weight and density; effects of forces; moments; energy, work and power; pressure.

NoteTopic 2: Thermal Physics

Kinetic particle model of matter; thermal properties and temperature; transfer of thermal energy (conduction, convection, radiation).

NoteTopic 3: Waves

General properties of waves; light (reflection, refraction, thin converging lenses); electromagnetic spectrum; sound.

NoteTopic 4: Electricity & Magnetism

Simple phenomena of magnetism; electrical quantities; electric circuits and electrical safety; electromagnetic effects.

NoteTopic 5: Nuclear Physics

The nuclear model of the atom; the nucleus; radioactivity (alpha, beta, gamma emissions, half-life, safety).

NoteTopic 6: Space Physics

The Solar System; stars and the Universe (Hubble constant, redshift, Big Bang theory).

NoteHigh School Mathematics Study Guidance: Pre-Calculus & Calculus Foundations

Welcome to the Common Core Pre-Calculus & Calculus Foundations portal! Pre-Calculus serves as the critical bridge connecting advanced algebra and geometry with university-level AP Calculus (AB/BC) and STEM higher education.

Below you will find 162 curated printable practice worksheets organized into two dedicated tabs: - Pre-Calculus Worksheets: 93 topic-focused worksheets across 14 units (functions, trigonometry, vectors, polar coordinates, matrices, conic sections, combinatorics, and series). - Calculus Worksheets: 69 topic-focused worksheets across 8 units (limits, continuity, differentiation, applications of derivatives, integration, definite integrals, differential equations).

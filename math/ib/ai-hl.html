1. Course Introduction

Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation (AI) Higher Level (HL) focuses on the practical applications of mathematics in real-world contexts, modeling economic and physical systems, and advanced statistics and probability. It emphasizes the extensive use of technology and graphing software to solve complex, data-driven problems. It is highly recommended for students planning to pursue Economics, Finance, Data Science, Business, or Applied Sciences at top universities.

Assessment Outline

The IB Mathematics AI HL course is assessed through three final written examinations (80%) and an independent mathematical exploration task (20%). A graphic display calculator (GDC) is required for all written examinations:

Assessment Component Duration Calculator Policy Marks Weighting
Paper 1 (Short Questions) 120 minutes (2 hours) Calculator required (GDC required) 110 marks 30%
Paper 2 (Long Questions) 120 minutes (2 hours) Calculator required (GDC required) 110 marks 30%
Paper 3 (Problem Solving) 60 minutes (1 hour) Calculator required (GDC required) 55 marks 20%
Internal Assessment (IA) - Exploration Independent research (12-20 pages) No restrictions 20 marks 20%

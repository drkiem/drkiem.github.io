1. Course Introduction

Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (AA) Higher Level (HL) focuses on developing strong analytical and rational thinking skills. The course emphasizes rigorous mathematical proofs, algebraic methods, and deep concepts of calculus and algebraic analysis. It is highly recommended for students aiming to study Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, or Mathematics at premier universities worldwide.

Assessment Outline

The IB Mathematics AA HL course is assessed through final written examinations (80%) and an independent mathematical exploration task (20%). A graphic display calculator (GDC) is required only for Paper 2 and Paper 3:

Assessment Component Duration Calculator Policy Marks Weighting
Paper 1 (Section A & B) 120 minutes (2 hours) Non-calculator (Non-GDC) 110 marks 30%
Paper 2 (Section A & B) 120 minutes (2 hours) Calculator allowed (GDC required) 110 marks 30%
Paper 3 (Problem Solving) 60 minutes (1 hour) Calculator allowed (GDC required) 55 marks 20%
Internal Assessment (IA) - Exploration Independent research (12-20 pages) No restrictions 20 marks 20%

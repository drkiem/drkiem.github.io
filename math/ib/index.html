IB Mathematics Overview

International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme Mathematics

NoteLearning Guidance & Exam Preparation: IB Mathematics

Welcome to the specialized IB Mathematics learning portal. We provide comprehensive study guides, practice questions, and past papers dynamically mapped for both the Analysis & Approaches (AA) and Applications & Interpretation (AI) pathways, at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL).

Choose Your Learning Pathway

To get started, select your specific course below. Each page links directly to detailed study notes, syllabus checklists, topic-based worksheets, and actual exam past papers.

Math AA HL

Analysis & Approaches (Higher Level) * Designed for students intending to study engineering, mathematics, physics, or computer science. * Emphasizes theoretical mathematical proofs, algebraic structures, and strong non-calculator skills. * Explore Resources →

Math AA SL

Analysis & Approaches (Standard Level) * Recommended for students pursuing biochemistry, economics, or general natural sciences. * Focuses on analytical algebraic skills, functions, trigonometry, and calculus with high academic rigor. * Explore Resources →

Math AI HL

Applications & Interpretation (Higher Level) * Ideal for students targeting economics, finance, data science, or social science statistics. * Heavy emphasis on technology and graphing display calculators (GDC), matrices, probability, and complex mathematical modeling. * Explore Resources →

Math AI SL

Applications & Interpretation (Standard Level) * Suitable for students intending to study humanities, arts, design, or business requiring basic data analysis. * Focuses on utilizing GDC tools to solve real-world statistical and financial problems. * Explore Resources →

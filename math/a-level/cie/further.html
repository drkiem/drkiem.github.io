CIE A-Level Further Mathematics

Cambridge International AS & A Level Further Mathematics

Keywords

Cambridge International A Level Further Mathematics 9231, CIE 9231 Further Math, A Level Further Mathematics past papers, Further Pure Mathematics 1, Further Pure Mathematics 2, Further Mechanics, Further Probability and Statistics, 9231 question papers mark schemes

NoteAcademic Portal: Cambridge International AS & A Level Further Mathematics (9231)

Welcome to the official study portal for Cambridge International AS & A Level Further Mathematics (9231). This rigorous, higher-level curriculum is designed for high-achieving mathematics students preparing for university degrees in Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering, and Theoretical Physics at elite institutions worldwide (such as Cambridge, Oxford, Imperial, MIT, and Ivy League universities).

1. Course Introduction

Cambridge International AS & A Level Further Mathematics (9231) extends mathematical understanding beyond standard A-Level Mathematics (9709), introducing advanced university-level concepts including Proof by Mathematical Induction, Linear Spaces, Matrix Transformations, Polar Coordinates, Complex Numbers, Second-order Differential Equations, Non-linear Mechanics, and Advanced Inferential Statistics.

Assessment Structure

The 9231 qualification comprises four specialized papers. Candidates can achieve either a Cambridge International AS Level (2 papers) or a full Cambridge International A Level (all 4 papers).

Calculator permitted. 30%

Paper 3: Further Mechanics 1 hr 30 mins
50 marks		 Motion of a Projectile, Equilibrium of a Rigid Body, Circular Motion, Hooke’s Law (Elastic Strings & Springs), Linear Motion under a Variable Force, Momentum in 2D (Oblique Elastic/Inelastic Collisions). Calculator permitted. 20%
(40% of AS)
Paper 4: Further Probability & Statistics 1 hr 30 mins
50 marks		 Continuous Random Variables, Sampling & Estimation ($t$-distribution), $\chi^2$-Tests for Independence and Goodness of Fit, Non-parametric Tests (Single-sample Sign Test, Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test), Probability Generating Functions (PGFs). Calculator permitted. 20%
(40% of AS)

Routes to Certification

Candidates can choose between two official Cambridge assessment routes depending on their academic pathways:

Certification Route Required Paper Combinations Assessment Details & Progression
AS Level Further Mathematics
2 Papers		 Option 1: Paper 1 (FP1) + Paper 3 (FM)
Option 2: Paper 1 (FP1) + Paper 4 (FS)		 Awarded with grades A through E. Provides foundational knowledge in university-level pure mathematics alongside either mechanics or advanced statistics.
Full A Level Further Mathematics
All 4 Papers		 Compulsory 4-Paper Linear / Staged:
Paper 1 (FP1) + Paper 2 (FP2) + Paper 3 (FM) + Paper 4 (FS)		 Awarded with grades A* through E. Candidates can sit all 4 papers in a single terminal exam series, or carry forward their AS Level results from Year 1 to complete the full A-Level.

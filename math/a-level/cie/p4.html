CIE Mechanics

Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics (9709)

Note

Welcome, students! This academic portal provides unit-specific study materials, syllabus outlines, and exam revision resources to help you master P4 and prepare for your modular exams.

1. Syllabus

The detailed syllabus structure for CIE Mechanics (Paper 4) comprises the following topics:

4.1 Forces and equilibrium
4.2 Kinematics of motion in a straight line
4.3 Momentum
4.4 Newton’s laws of motion
4.5 Energy, work and power

Welcome to the specialized study and revision portal for Cambridge IGCSE International Mathematics (0607). Designed specifically for students at premier international schools, this syllabus integrates rigorous mathematical reasoning with modern Graphic Display Calculator (GDC) proficiency and Mathematical Investigation & Modelling (Papers 5 & 6). This course serves as the most effective academic gateway to the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Mathematics (Analysis and Approaches / Applications and Interpretation) and Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics.

1. Course Introduction & Qualification Framework

Cambridge IGCSE International Mathematics (0607) provides a contemporary and global mathematical curriculum. It places equal emphasis on theoretical mathematical principles, computational mastery through graphic display calculators, and autonomous inquiry through open-ended investigations and real-world modelling tasks.

Assessment Structure & Paper Configurations

The qualification offers two distinct assessment pathways: Core Tier (targeted at grades C–G) and Extended Tier (targeted at grades A*–E). Candidates must sit three written papers within their chosen tier.

Paper Tier Assessment Outline & Calculator Policy Duration & Marks Weighting

Paper 2 (Extended) Extended Structured short-answer questions assessing rigorous mathematical thinking, non-calculator arithmetic, surds, indices, and exact trigonometry. No calculator permitted. 45 mins
40 marks 		20%
Paper 4 (Extended) Extended Advanced multi-step problems requiring full synthesis of the Extended curriculum and deep utilization of graphic calculator functions (graph analysis, polynomial extrema, non-linear regression). Graphic Display Calculator (GDC) required. 2 hrs 15 mins
120 marks 		60%
Paper 6 (Extended) Extended Two mandatory sections: Section A (Mathematical Investigation) and Section B (Mathematical Modelling). Assesses independent mathematical formulation, justification, and real-world scenario analysis. GDC required. 1 hr 40 mins
60 marks 		20%

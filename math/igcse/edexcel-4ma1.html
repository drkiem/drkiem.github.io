1. Course Introduction

Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (4MA1) is a structured and comprehensive academic curriculum designed to develop mathematical skills, logical thinking, and problem-solving capabilities. The syllabus is divided into two tiers of entry: Foundation Tier (grades 5–1) and Higher Tier (grades 9–4, with grade 3 allowed), offering appropriate routes of assessment for students of different abilities.

Assessment Outline

Paper Number Tier Assessment Information Marks Weighting
Paper 1F Foundation Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Pearson. The paper is weighted at 50% of the qualification, targeted at grades 5–1. Calculator allowed. 100 marks 50%
Paper 2F Foundation Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Pearson. The paper is weighted at 50% of the qualification, targeted at grades 5–1. Calculator allowed. 100 marks 50%
Paper 1H Higher Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Pearson. The paper is weighted at 50% of the qualification, targeted at grades 9–4 with 3 allowed. Calculator allowed. 100 marks 50%
Paper 2H Higher Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Pearson. The paper is weighted at 50% of the qualification, targeted at grades 9–4 with 3 allowed. Calculator allowed. 100 marks 50%

Assessment Objectives (AOs) Weightings: * AO1 (Number and algebra): 57 - 63% * AO2 (Shape, space and measures): 22 - 28% * AO3 (Handling data): 12 - 18%

1 Course Introduction

Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) is a structured and comprehensive academic curriculum designed to develop mathematical skills, logical thinking, and problem-solving capabilities. The syllabus is divided into two tiers of entry: Core Tier (eligible for grades C–G) and Extended Tier (eligible for grades A*–E), offering appropriate routes of assessment for students of different abilities.

Assessment Outline

Paper Number Tier Assessment Information Marks Weighting
Paper 1 (Core) Core Assessed through a 1 hour 30 minutes examination set and marked by Cambridge. Use of a calculator is not allowed. Weighted at 50% of the qualification, eligible for grades C to G. 80 marks 50%
Paper 3 (Core) Core Assessed through a 1 hour 30 minutes examination set and marked by Cambridge. A scientific calculator is required. Weighted at 50% of the qualification, eligible for grades C to G. 80 marks 50%
Paper 2 (Extended) Extended Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Cambridge. Use of a calculator is not allowed. Weighted at 50% of the qualification, eligible for grades A* to E. 100 marks 50%
Paper 4 (Extended) Extended Assessed through a 2-hour examination set and marked by Cambridge. A scientific calculator is required. Weighted at 50% of the qualification, eligible for grades A* to E. 100 marks 50%

Assessment Objectives (AOs) Weightings: * AO1 (Knowledge and understanding of mathematical techniques): Core 60–70% | Extended 40–50% * AO2 (Analyse, interpret and communicate mathematically): Core 30–40% | Extended 50–60%

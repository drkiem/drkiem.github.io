IGCSE Mathematics Overview

International General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE) Mathematics Curriculums

NoteSolid Foundation for Transition to IB / A-Level

Welcome, secondary students! The IGCSE Mathematics program plays an essential role in training logical mathematical thinking, mastering fundamental algebraic manipulations, and building academic English vocabulary. NKK-Education Hub provides detailed exam preparation resources and past papers for the most popular pathways: CIE 0580, CIE 0607 (International), and Pearson Edexcel 4MA1.

Choose Your Exam Pathway

Select your specific exam curriculum below to go directly to its dedicated portal, featuring past papers, marking schemes, worked solutions, and key formula sheets:

CIE IGCSE Mathematics (0580)

Core Cambridge IGCSE Math Curriculum * Focus: Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Coordinate Geometry, Probability & Statistics. Categorized into Core (target grade C) and Extended (target grades A* to B). * Explore Resources (0580) →

CIE International Mathematics (0607)

Cambridge IGCSE International Math * Tailored for international schools incorporating Graphic Display Calculators (GDC) early. Focuses on technology-integrated problem solving and mathematical investigation. * Explore Resources (0607) →

Edexcel IGCSE Mathematics A (4MA1)

Pearson Edexcel IGCSE Math * Highly structured syllabus focusing on practical mathematical applications and strong pure algebra skills. Divided into Foundation and Higher tiers. * Explore Resources (4MA1) →

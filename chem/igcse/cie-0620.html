1. Course Introduction

CIE IGCSE Chemistry (Syllabus Code: 0620) provides students with a detailed and comprehensive understanding of the technological world in which they live, and an informed interest in science and scientific developments. Students learn about the basic principles of chemistry through a mix of theoretical study and practical investigations, developing essential skills for further academic pathways (such as A-Level or IB DP Chemistry).

Assessment Outline

The CIE IGCSE Chemistry 0620 course is assessed through three papers. Candidates who have studied the Core syllabus content are entered for Paper 1, Paper 3 and either Paper 5 or Paper 6. Candidates who have studied the Extended syllabus content (Core and Supplement) are entered for Paper 2, Paper 4 and either Paper 5 or Paper 6.

Assessment Paper Target Curriculum Format & Duration Marks Weighting
Paper 1 (Multiple Choice) Core 40 multiple-choice questions — 45 minutes 40 marks 30%
Paper 2 (Multiple Choice) Extended 40 multiple-choice questions — 45 minutes 40 marks 30%
Paper 3 (Theory) Core Short-answer and structured questions — 75 minutes 80 marks 50%
Paper 4 (Theory) Extended Short-answer and structured questions — 75 minutes 80 marks 50%
Paper 5 (Practical Test) Core & Extended Laboratory-based practical tasks — 75 minutes 40 marks 20%
Paper 6 (Alternative to Practical) Core & Extended Written questions on experimental skills — 60 minutes 40 marks 20%

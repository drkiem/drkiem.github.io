1. COURSE OVERVIEW & SYLLABUS

Cambridge International AS & A Level Chemistry (Syllabus Code: 9701) provides a comprehensive foundation in physical, inorganic, and organic chemistry. The course is structured into two main stages: AS Level (typically taken in the first year) and A2 Level (taken in the second year).

Assessment Outline (Papers 1 to 5)

The complete A-Level qualification consists of five examination papers. The table below summarizes the structure, duration, and weighting of each paper:

Level Paper / Component Duration Question Type Marks Weight (A-Level)
AS Level
(50% of A-Level) 		Paper 1 (Multiple Choice) 1h 15m 40 multiple-choice questions 40 15.5%
Paper 2 (AS Structured Questions) 1h 15m Structured theory questions 60 23%
Paper 3 (Advanced Practical Skills) 2 hours Laboratory practical assessment 40 11.5%
A2 Level
(50% of A-Level) 		Paper 4 (A Level Structured Questions) 2 hours Structured theory questions on A2 topics 100 38%
Paper 5 (Planning, Analysis & Evaluation) 1h 15m Written practical skills theory exam 30 12%

1. Course Overview & Syllabus

Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level (IAL) Chemistry provides a modular, flexible assessment structure designed to build deep conceptual understanding and practical analytical skills across physical, inorganic, and organic chemistry.

Assessment Outline (Units 1 to 6)

The complete IAL Chemistry qualification consists of six modular examination units divided equally between IAS (Year 1) and IA2 (Year 2):

Level Unit / Component Duration Assessment Format Marks Weight (A-Level)
IAS Level
(50% of A-Level) 		Unit 1 (WCH11)
Structure, Bonding & Intro Organic Chemistry 		1h 30m Written paper (Section A: MCQs, Section B: Structured) 80 20%
Unit 2 (WCH12)
Energetics, Group 2 & 7, Redox & Organic II 		1h 30m Written paper (Section A: MCQs, Section B: Structured) 80 20%
Unit 3 (WCH13)
Practical Skills in Chemistry I 		1h 20m Written practical skills exam (Laboratory Techniques) 50 10%
IA2 Level
(50% of A-Level) 		Unit 4 (WCH14)
Rates, Equilibria & Further Organic Chemistry 		1h 45m Written paper (Structured, Data & Mechanism questions) 90 20%
Unit 5 (WCH15)
Transition Metals & Organic Nitrogen Chemistry 		1h 45m Written paper (Structured, Transition Metal & Synthesis) 90 20%
Unit 6 (WCH16)
Practical Skills in Chemistry II 		1h 20m Written practical skills exam (Advanced Analysis) 50 10%

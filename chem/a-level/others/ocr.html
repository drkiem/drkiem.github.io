OCR A-Level Chemistry A (Specification Codes: H032 / H432)

NoteOCR A-Level Chemistry Learning Dashboard

Welcome to the OCR A-Level Chemistry study center. Access official specification documents, module-by-module revision guides, flashcards, practical skills handbooks (PAGs), and topical practice question packs with mark schemes across Modules 1 to 6.

1. Course Overview & Syllabus

The OCR A-Level Chemistry A specification (H032 for AS Level, H432 for A-Level) features a modular structure divided into six core modules: Practical Skills, Foundations in Chemistry, Periodic Table and Energy, Core Organic Chemistry, Physical Chemistry and Transition Elements, and Organic Chemistry and Analysis.

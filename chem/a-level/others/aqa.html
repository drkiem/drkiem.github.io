1. COURSE OVERVIEW & SYLLABUS

The AQA A-Level Chemistry specification (7404 for AS Level, 7405 for A-Level) features a linear structure with three key subject areas: Physical Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, and Organic Chemistry, alongside 12 Required Practicals.

Assessment Outline (7404 & 7405)
